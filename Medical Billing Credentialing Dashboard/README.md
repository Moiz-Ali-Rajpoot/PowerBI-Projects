# 🏥 Medical Billing Credentialing Sign-Off Dashboard — Power BI

> **Credentialing Operations Intelligence** — Built with Microsoft Excel as the data source and Power BI for tracking credentialing submissions, approvals, and team activity across practices and contracts.

---

## 🗂️ Overview

This Power BI dashboard provides a comprehensive operational view of the **Medical Billing Credentialing Sign-Off** process. It tracks active users, total practices, contracts, submissions, and approvals — all in one centralized interface.

The dashboard is divided into two views:

- **Overview** — High-level KPIs including Total Contracts (3,386), Total Submissions (2,730), and Total Approvals (909) across 396 practices with 18 active users. Includes a heatmap of credentialing activity by week and month, a donut chart breaking down follow-up activity types (Email Follow-Ups, Calls Follow-Up, Grid Submission, Portal Updation), monthly submission trends, and a per-user activity summary ranking both active and inactive team members by total submissions.

- **Daily Progress** — A drill-down view for day-level monitoring of credentialing tasks, enabling supervisors to track team output, identify bottlenecks, and ensure timely sign-offs.

## 💡 Purpose & Business Value

This dashboard was designed to support:

- **Credentialing Teams** — Monitor individual and team-level submission progress in real time
- **Operations Management** — Track contract and practice volumes, identify inactive users, and measure approval rates
- **Supervisors & Team Leads** — Use weekly heatmaps and daily progress views to manage workload distribution
- **Executives** — Get a quick snapshot of overall credentialing pipeline health and throughput
